Sentinel Passport Photo

Load a photo, mark the top of the head and the chin, and get a passport, visa or ID photo at the exact size, plus a sheet to print. It runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded.

Zoom: scroll wheel, two-finger pinch, the slider or the + and − buttons. Move the photo: drag it.

  1. Choose your photo. Look straight at the camera, plain background.
  2. Drag the two markers: the top of the head (crown) and the bottom of the chin.
  3. Press Auto-fit. The page zooms and centres the head to the size the document allows.
  4. Fine-tune with zoom and drag, then download.

Print sheet

Tiles copies of your photo with cut guides, ready for a photo printer at 300 dpi.

Check before you submit. Sizes and head-height ranges are the published guides as I know them, and they change. Confirm them with the issuing authority. This tool does not judge lighting, expression, glasses or background. The photo it starts with is a drawn sample, not a real face.
