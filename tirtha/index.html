Tirtha: Sacred Places of India

The traditional circuits of pilgrimage, on a map: the twelve Jyotirlingas, the Char Dham, the Chota Char Dham in the Himalaya, the seven Saptapuri cities, the five Pancha Bhoota Sthalams and the six abodes of Murugan. Tap a marker for the deity, the place and the river beside it.

Circuits follow the widely used traditional lists. Some lists are disputed: three Jyotirlinga sites (Baidyanath, Nageshwar and, in some accounts, Bhimashankar) are claimed by more than one place, and this page shows one and says so. Coordinates come from Wikipedia and OpenStreetMap and place the marker at the temple or town, not surveyed to the metre. The Shakti Peethas and the 108 Divya Desams are not here yet because the lists differ between sources and are to be added from a single cited one. Map tiles © OpenStreetMap contributors.
