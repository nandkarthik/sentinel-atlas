Daily Tithi, Nakshatra, Yoga, Karana and Vara for Andhra Pradesh and the United States, computed live from sun and moon position — plus the day's Rahu Kalam, Yamagandam, Gulika Kalam, Abhijit Muhurta and Brahma Muhurtam.

 
Ekadashi Purnima Amavasya Sankashti / Pradosham / Shivaratri

    Festival dates follow the Telugu (Amanta) lunar calendar and are computed, not looked up, so a festival whose tithi begins or ends close to the deciding time may differ by a day from your local Panchangam. Each day shows the Tithi (and Nakshatra) in effect at sunrise, the traditional way of naming a day. S = Shukla (waxing), K = Krishna (waning). Tap a day for its full timings.

    Auspicious & Inauspicious Windows

      Full Timeline

      Ordered by time of day. A day can show more than one Tithi, Nakshatra, Yoga or Karana, since each one's real length rarely lines up with the calendar day.

        Sun/moon positions use low-precision public-domain formulas (NOAA/Meeus sunrise equation; four-term lunar theory), accurate to roughly a minute for sunrise/sunset and 15–30 minutes for Tithi/Nakshatra/Yoga/Karana boundaries — not scientific-grade, but consistent with what most printed Panchangams target.
