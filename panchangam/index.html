    Full Timeline

    Ordered by time of day. A day can show more than one Tithi, Nakshatra, Yoga or Karana, since each one's real length rarely lines up with the calendar day.

      Sun/moon positions use low-precision public-domain formulas (NOAA/Meeus sunrise equation; four-term lunar theory), accurate to roughly a minute for sunrise/sunset and 15–30 minutes for Tithi/Nakshatra/Yoga/Karana boundaries — not scientific-grade, but consistent with what most printed Panchangams target. Lahiri ayanamsa used for sidereal quantities.
