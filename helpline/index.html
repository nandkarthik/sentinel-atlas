Sahaya: Helpline Numbers

The numbers worth having ready, for India and the United States. On a phone, tap a number to call it. Emergency numbers are shaded.

State pages list local numbers only. The national numbers (112, 100, 1091, 1098, 1930 and the rest) work in every state and are under India.

Check before relying on it. These numbers are listed from memory of public sources and are not re-checked against official sites. The state numbers are the most likely to change, so confirm on your bill or the official site before depending on one. Other states, and other cities in these three, are not listed yet.
