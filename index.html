Interactive references and long-form reading, one region at a time. It starts in India — more territory gets added as it's mapped.

PLATE I

Bharat Chronicle

An interactive timeline of Indian history, from the Indus Valley to today, with the standing threads across the eras, alongside the full verbatim text of the Constitution of India — 494 articles and 12 schedules.

Itihasa & Purana

The Ramayana and Mahabharata with their family trees, and the Hindu pantheon — Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva, the Devi and their families — with portraits and a map of major temples.

 INDIA · EPICS + PANTHEON → PLATE III

Sentinel Panchangam

A live Hindu daily almanac for Andhra Pradesh — Tithi, Nakshatra, Yoga, Karana and Vara computed from sun and moon position, plus Rahu Kalam, Yamagandam, Gulika Kalam, Abhijit Muhurta and Brahma Muhurtam for any date and city.

 INDIA · ALMANAC → PLATE IV

Sentinel Money

A plain-English glossary of 90 investing, home-buying, credit and tax terms, each with an example and a quiz, plus mortgage, compound-interest and retirement calculators.

 FINANCE · GLOSSARY + TOOLS → PLATE V

Nadi: Rivers of India

A map of India's rivers with animated flow from source to sea, an encyclopedia entry for each, and the dams, barrages, canals and river links built on them.

 INDIA · RIVERS + MAP → PLATE VI

Maap: Units and Conversions

Convert land, weight, volume, distance, time, speed, energy, data, pressure, power and cooking measures between metric, US and Indian units, and read big numbers as crore, lakh, million and billion.

 MEASURES · CONVERTER → PLATE VII

Sahaya: Helpline Numbers

The emergency and helpline numbers worth having ready, for India (with Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala and Maharashtra) and the United States: police, fire, ambulance, poison, crisis, women and children, road and consumer help.
